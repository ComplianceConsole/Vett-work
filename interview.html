Questions
FAQ
Do I need special equipment?
Just your laptop or phone camera. A window for natural light and a quiet room. That's it. Most built-in cameras are more than good enough.
Can I re-record my answers?
Yes — unlimited retakes. Watch it back, and either use it or record again. You can also swap out individual answers without redoing all six.
What if I don't like the AI questions?
Each question has a "Try a different question" button — 3 attempts per question, each staying on the same theme so your answers stay coherent.
Can employers see my profile without my permission?
No. Your profile is private by default. You control visibility. When you apply for a role, only that employer sees your profile. Change settings any time.
How is this different from a LinkedIn video intro?
LinkedIn video is one generic clip. vett.work gives you 6 structured interview answers personalised to your background — not a selfie, a virtual interview.
What is a role-specific interview?
Paste any job ad URL. AI reads the role and your CV, then writes 3 tailored questions for that exact job. You record 3 answers and attach them to that specific application. One is included free.
What's the refund policy?
Once your questions have been generated the service has been delivered. If you have a technical issue preventing you from recording, email hello@vett.work and we'll sort it out.
