Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025 · vett.work is operated by [COMPANY NAME] ABN [ABN NUMBER]

This is a plain English summary: we collect the information you give us to run the platform. We don't sell your data. Candidates control whether employers can see their profile. We store your data securely and you can delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

vett.work is a job platform connecting candidates and employers across Australia and New Zealand. It is operated by [COMPANY NAME], ABN [ABN NUMBER], based in Brisbane, Australia.

For privacy enquiries contact us at hello@vett.work.

2. What information we collect

Candidates

  • Name, email address, and location
  • CV or LinkedIn URL you provide
  • Text extracted from your CV for AI processing
  • Video recordings you create on the platform
  • Documents you upload (police checks, licences, certifications etc)
  • Payment information (processed by Stripe — we do not store card details)
  • Profile preferences and visibility settings

Employers

  • Name, email address, and company details
  • Job listings you post
  • Payment information (processed by Stripe)
  • Notes and shortlist data related to candidates

3. How we use your information

  • To operate the platform and provide the services you've paid for
  • To generate personalised interview questions from your CV using AI
  • To match candidates with relevant employers (if you opt in)
  • To process payments via Stripe
  • To send transactional emails (account confirmation, receipts)
  • To improve the platform

4. Who we share your information with

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

We share information with:

  • Employers on vett.work — only if you opt in to the candidate database, or if you apply for a role. You control this.
  • Stripe — for payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data.
  • Supabase — our database and file storage provider. Data is stored in secure cloud infrastructure.
  • Mux — our video hosting provider. Your video clips are stored and streamed via Mux.
  • Anthropic — your CV text is sent to Anthropic's API to generate interview questions. It is not used to train their models.

5. Candidate profile visibility

Your profile is private by default. Employers cannot see your profile unless you explicitly opt in to the candidate database in your dashboard settings. You can change this at any time.

When you apply for a role, the employer who posted that role can see the profile information you submitted with your application.

6. Video recordings

Your video clips are stored securely via Mux. They are only shared with employers you authorise — either by opting in to the candidate database or by applying for a specific role.

You can delete your video clips from your dashboard at any time.

7. Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent to data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)

To exercise any of these rights email hello@vett.work.

8. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or financial compliance purposes.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security practices including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure cloud infrastructure, and row-level security on our database. No system is completely secure — if you believe your account has been compromised please contact us immediately.

10. Cookies

We use cookies and local storage to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify you of significant changes via email. Continued use of the platform after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact
